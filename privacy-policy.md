## A little clarity, before you play.

This Privacy Policy explains how **gillanitech** handles information when you use **Bubble Pop: Reef Rescue** (Android package `com.tidelight.pearlbound`) and this privacy website.

The game does not require an account. Your progress and game preferences are saved on your device. Advertising services can process device and usage information separately, as explained below.

## What stays on your device. What reaches a service.

### Game progress and preferences

The game stores unlocked levels, star ratings, coins, booster inventory, rescued characters, daily rewards, tutorial progress, and music, sound and vibration preferences locally. A local backup of the previous save may also be kept. These saves are not uploaded to a gillanitech game server by the current app.

### Advertising information

The game integrates Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). When these advertising services are used, Google’s SDK automatically collects and shares IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app/ad interaction information, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account identifiers where available. Google uses these for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The data and permitted uses can depend on your consent choices, device settings and SDK configuration. Advertising data is separate from your locally saved game progress and personal audio files.

### Information you send to support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information. We use your message to investigate and respond to your request.

### Information we do not ask you to provide

The current game does not include account registration, a chat service, or a feature that asks for your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise GPS location. It does not process card details or include real-money in-app purchases in its current implementation.

## Your music. Your choice. On your device.

My Music lets you choose an audio file through Android’s system file picker and play it during the game. It is optional: you can keep using the original game music without selecting a file. No Spotify account, streaming-service connection or microphone recording is part of this feature.

### What the game accesses and remembers

The game requests read access to the specific audio file you select. It uses the file’s display name and Android file reference (URI), and requests persistent read access where the file provider supports it so that your selection can be reopened next time. The selected file reference, display name, music volume and loop preference are stored in the local game save; a previous-save backup may retain an earlier selection.

The game plays the selected audio through Android’s media player. gillanitech does not upload your selected audio, its file reference or display name to a game server, and does not send them to the advertising SDK through the My Music feature. The game does not scan your music library or request access to all your files, and it does not modify or delete the selected audio file.

### Files from cloud providers

Android’s picker can show local files and documents from installed cloud-storage providers. If you select a cloud document, that provider may download or stream it and process information under its own privacy policy. File availability and supported formats depend on Android and the provider. My Music does not bypass DRM or unlock downloads held inside subscription music apps.

### How to stop or remove a selection

Open My Music using the music-note shortcut on the map or gameplay screen, or through Settings. You can pause playback, change volume and looping, or choose **Use original music** to clear the active selection and release its retained read permission where supported. This does not delete your original audio file. An earlier file reference or name can remain in the local previous-save backup until it is replaced or you clear the app’s data. Clearing app data removes the game’s stored references and preferences; it does not erase audio files stored elsewhere on your device or in your cloud account.

Where required, a consent message is presented before eligible ad requests. You can revisit available choices through **Settings → Ad privacy choices** when that option is required and available for your region. You can also review Android’s advertising-ID controls in your device settings.

Declining personalization does not necessarily stop every ad or every form of data processing. Limited or non-personalized advertising may still use information for delivery, measurement or fraud prevention. See [Google’s advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

gillanitech does not operate a cloud account or cloud-save service for this game, and does not sell your local game progress. Advertising-related sharing is described above and is subject to applicable choices and law.

## Practical steps, without an account.

### Remove local game data

Open Android **Settings → Apps → Bubble Pop: Reef Rescue → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data**. Labels vary by phone. This removes progress and settings from that installation. Removing the app normally removes its local storage, but device backups may restore it later.

### Remove your music selection

Open **My Music → Use original music**. To remove any earlier selection kept in the previous-save backup as well, clear the app’s storage. Your original audio file remains with its device or cloud provider.

### Review advertising choices

Use the in-game ad privacy option when available, and review your Android advertising-ID settings. Provider-held records are separate from your local game save.
